# Section 7.7 Training-Budget Sensitivity (Extended Budget Robustness Check)

Evidence markers used in this draft: B1 = `PAPER_STAGE6_EXTENDED_BUDGET_3090_REPORT.md`; B2 = `PAPER_STAGE6_EXTENDED_BUDGET_ANALYSIS.md`; A1 = main_table_metrics.csv; A2 = low_snr_table.csv; P1 = `section4_experimental_protocol.md`; R1 = `section5_1_overall_fixed_split_results.md`; R2 = `section5_2_low_snr_fusion_tradeoff.md`; L1 = `section7_limitations.md`.

## 7.7 Training-Budget Sensitivity

This subsection records a separate-evidence-label robustness check for the training-budget assumption used in Stage 5A. It is included under Section 7 Limitations because it does not modify, replace, or re-aggregate the main Stage 5A/5B evidence; it only constrains how that evidence should be interpreted. The check was registered under the evidence label `EXTENDED_BUDGET_3090` and a separate output root `results/paper_stage6/extended_budget_3090/`, so the Stage 5A main table, the Stage 5A low-SNR table, and the Stage 5A paired statistical tests remain unchanged. Evidence: P1, B1, B2, L1.

The check trained four of the nine Stage 5A model rows under an extended budget on the same fixed split and the same three training seeds. The four models were `cldnn`, `resnet1d`, `iq_param_matched`, and `fusion_iq_stft`, selected because they cover the strongest stable I/Q baseline, the second I/Q baseline, the parameter-matched fusion control, and the static fusion model whose best Stage 5A epoch was closest to the original budget cap. The five remaining Stage 5A model rows (`cnn1d`, `tfcnn_stft`, `mcldnn`, `lwamcnet`, `gated_fusion_iq_stft`) were not included in this robustness check; their main-table interpretation continues to come exclusively from Stage 5A/5B. Evidence: B1, B2, R1.

The protocol changed in three controlled dimensions relative to Stage 5A. The training epoch cap was lifted from 20 to 50, with a 5-epoch linear warmup followed by cosine annealing applied to the AdamW learning rate. The early-stop patience was lifted from 6 to 15 to allow the cosine schedule to complete before stopping. The GPU hardware also changed from RTX 4070 (Ada, sm_89) to RTX 3090 (Ampere, sm_86) under the same NVIDIA driver `570.211.01`, CUDA `12.8`, and PyTorch `2.9.1+cu128`. The hardware change is registered as a confound in the analysis addendum and prevents a direct paired statistical comparison between the two evidence labels at the prediction level. Evidence: B1, B2, P1.

The aggregate overall-accuracy deltas were small. For `cldnn`, the extended-budget mean across three seeds was 0.6145, against the Stage 5A mean 0.6129, for a delta of +0.0015. For `resnet1d`, 0.5963 against 0.5959, delta +0.0004. For `iq_param_matched`, 0.6011 against 0.5945, delta +0.0067. For `fusion_iq_stft`, 0.5851 against 0.5771, delta +0.0080. All four overall-accuracy deltas are within plus or minus one percentage point, and the within-table ranking observed in Stage 5A is preserved: `cldnn` remains the strongest of the four, and `fusion_iq_stft` remains the weakest of the four. Evidence: B1, B2, A1, R1.

The low-SNR result is more informative than the overall result. None of the four models gained low-SNR accuracy under the extended budget. The four mean low-SNR deltas were `cldnn` -0.0003, `resnet1d` -0.0005, `iq_param_matched` -0.0021, and `fusion_iq_stft` -0.0089. The corresponding mid- and high-SNR deltas were positive, ranging from +0.002 to +0.022. Within this evidence set, the extended budget moves accuracy from low-SNR to mid- and high-SNR examples rather than producing a uniform improvement, and the largest movement appears on `fusion_iq_stft`, which is the model whose Stage 5A interpretation in Section 5.2 already characterized a low-SNR trade-off. Evidence: B1, B2, A1, A2, R2.

The best-epoch distribution gives additional context for the budget assumption. Across the three seeds, `resnet1d` reached its best epoch at 15, 17, and 20, all inside or at the Stage 5A budget cap. `cldnn` reached its best epoch at 23, 24, and 27, slightly past the Stage 5A budget. `iq_param_matched` reached its best epoch at 21, 26, and 27, also slightly past the cap. `fusion_iq_stft` reached its best epoch at 34, 36, and 42, well past the cap. The pattern indicates that Stage 5A truncated training only marginally for `cldnn` and `iq_param_matched`, did not truncate `resnet1d` at all, and truncated `fusion_iq_stft` most aggressively. Yet the recovered overall accuracy for `fusion_iq_stft` is still about 3 percentage points below `cldnn`, so the fusion-versus-CLDNN gap is not closed by extending the budget alone. Evidence: B1, B2, R1, R2.

The supported interpretation of this robustness check is therefore narrow. The Stage 5A main-table overall ranking is not an artifact of an unrealistically short budget; it persists when the four most relevant models are retrained with a longer schedule and a different LR strategy on different GPU hardware. The Stage 5A low-SNR trade-off observation for `fusion_iq_stft` is also not refuted by extending the budget; the budget extension makes the trade-off slightly worse rather than better in this evidence set. The robustness check does not support claims about cross-architecture statistical equivalence at the prediction level, claims about the five excluded model rows, claims about CPU latency or FLOPs, or any RadioML2018.01A claim. Evidence: B1, B2, A1, A2, R1, R2, L1.

This subsection therefore extends the Section 7 limitations by adding a budget-sensitivity boundary: the conclusions in Section 5.1, Section 5.2, and Section 7.1 are robust to the specific training-budget choice used in Stage 5A under the conditions tested, while remaining bounded to the fixed RadioML2016.10A split, the registered seed set, and the original Stage 5A evidence label for any quantitative claim. Evidence: P1, R1, R2, L1, B1, B2.
